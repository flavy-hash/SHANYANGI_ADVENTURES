## Who we are

{company} is a tour operator based in Tanzania, organising safaris, Kilimanjaro climbs and Zanzibar holidays. When this policy says "we" or "us", it means {company}. You can reach us at any time using the details under "Contact us" below.

## Information you give us

We only collect what you choose to send us:

- **Trip requests and enquiries:** your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country, the trip you are interested in, travel dates, number of travellers, budget and any message you write.
- **Instant quotes on package pages:** your name, email address, phone number, country, travel date and number of adults and children, so we can prepare your quotation (PDF).
- **Live chat:** the messages you send, and your name and email address if you choose to give them.
- **Reviews:** your name, country, email address, travel month, the trip you took, your rating, your review and any photos you upload. Your email address is never published; we only use it if we need to check something about your review. Uploaded photos are re-processed on our server, which removes hidden data such as the GPS location many phones add to pictures.
- **Newsletter:** your email address.
- **When you book:** details we need to arrange your trip, such as the names of everyone travelling, passport details for permits and flights, arrival and departure information, dietary needs and any health information you decide to share with us (for example for a Kilimanjaro climb).

## Information collected automatically

To understand which pages and tours are popular, we keep a simple, anonymous visitor log:

- the page you visited, the tour you viewed, the date and time, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer), the website that sent you to us (for example a search engine) and your country;
- your country is looked up on our own server from your internet address, without sending it to anyone else, and **your internet (IP) address itself is not stored**;
- instead of a name or cookie, each visitor gets a random ID that **changes every day**, so we cannot follow you from one day to the next;
- if your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, nothing is recorded;
- these records are deleted automatically after {analytics_days} days.

## Cookies and similar technology

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. This website uses only:

- **essential cookies** that keep your session and protect our forms from misuse (they are needed for the site to work);
- a **language cookie** set only if you choose a language from our language menu, so the next page is translated too;
- a **chat key** stored in your browser (local storage) when you start a live chat, so you can continue the same conversation on another page. Clearing your browser data removes it.

## How we use your information

- to answer your questions and send you quotations;
- to plan, book and run your trip, including sharing the necessary details with the lodges, camps, airlines, park authorities and guides involved;
- to send our newsletter, only if you subscribed (every email lets you unsubscribe);
- to publish your review, only after we have checked it;
- to see, in summary form, how our website is used so we can improve it;
- to keep business and accounting records required by law.

We never sell your personal information.

## Services that receive information

Some parts of the website load content from other companies. Your browser then connects to them directly and they receive your internet address:

- **Google Translate**, loaded only when you choose a language from our menu;
- **map tiles** (OpenStreetMap, or Mapbox if enabled) when you view a route map on a package page;
- **image servers** that deliver some of the photos on our pages;
- **WhatsApp** (Meta), only when you click one of our WhatsApp buttons.

We also use a hosting provider for this website and an email provider to send and receive messages. Each of these companies handles data under its own privacy policy.

## How long we keep information

- enquiries, quotes and chat conversations: for as long as needed to help you and to keep normal business records;
- booking records: as long as tax and accounting laws require;
- the anonymous visitor log: {analytics_days} days;
- newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe;
- reviews: while they are published, or until you ask us to remove them.

## How we protect it

The website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to our admin area is limited to our team and protected by a password. Photos and videos are stored outside the public part of the website and shown through temporary links. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

## Your rights

- tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct anything that is wrong;
- delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
- stop sending you our newsletter or other messages;
- remove a review you wrote.

Contact us using the details below. We follow the Personal Data Protection Act of Tanzania, and we respect the equivalent rights of travellers from the European Union, the United Kingdom and elsewhere.
