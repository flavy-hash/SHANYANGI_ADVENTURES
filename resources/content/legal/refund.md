We know plans can change. This policy explains what happens if you cancel or change a trip booked with {company}, or if we have to. It forms part of our [Terms & Conditions]({terms_url}).

## How to cancel

Please cancel **in writing**, by email to {email}, quoting your booking or quotation reference. The date we receive your message is the date used to calculate any charges below.

## Deposits and costs we cannot recover

Your deposit secures your lodges, camps, vehicles and guides. If you cancel more than 60 days before arrival, the deposit may be refunded less any non-refundable supplier costs. Some costs cannot be recovered once paid on your behalf, such as domestic flights, Kilimanjaro and park permits, and lodges with strict cancellation terms.

## If you cancel

Depending on how long before your arrival date we receive your written cancellation, we may retain up to the following amounts:

| When you cancel | Amount we may retain |
|---|---|
| More than 60 days before arrival | Non-refundable supplier costs (the rest of your deposit may be refunded) |
| 31 to 60 days before arrival | Up to 50% of the total safari cost, depending on committed costs |
| 15 to 30 days before arrival | Up to 75% of the total safari cost |
| 14 days or less before arrival | Up to 100% where supplier charges are non-refundable or the safari cannot be resold |

The exact refund depends on the cancellation conditions of accommodation providers, airlines, parks, concession operators and other suppliers. We refund anything you have paid above the amount retained.

## Changing your dates

If you need to move your trip, tell us as early as possible. Changes made more than 60 days before arrival are usually possible without a charge from us, subject to availability; any extra costs from lodges, airlines or parks will be added. Changes requested later may be treated as a cancellation under the table above, although we will always try to help you find another option first.

## Unused services

There is no refund for any part of a trip you do not use once it has started, for example if you leave early, miss a game drive or excursion, or end a Kilimanjaro climb early. Extra nights or transport needed in those cases are at your own cost; your travel insurance may cover them.

## If we cancel or change your trip

- If we have to cancel your trip for a reason within our control, you can choose a full refund of everything you paid us or an alternative trip.
- If we make a significant change before you travel, you can accept the change, choose another trip, or cancel and receive a refund of what you paid us.
- If a trip has to be cancelled because of events beyond anyone's control (such as natural disasters, epidemics, government restrictions or unrest), we will offer new dates or a credit for a future trip, or refund what you paid minus costs we have already paid and cannot recover from our suppliers.

## How refunds are paid

We confirm the amount due within 7 days of your cancellation and aim to pay refunds within 14 working days after that. Refunds are processed after supplier payments and cancellation charges have been determined, and are paid by bank transfer or to the original payment method. Bank and payment-processing charges may be deducted, and changes in exchange rates are not covered by us.

## Travel insurance

Good travel insurance with cancellation cover can protect you if you need to cancel because of illness, injury or another unexpected event. We strongly recommend arranging it as soon as you book.
